# Caneyville.org

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Residents of Caneyville and nearby Grayson County looking for local businesses, school information, community updates, and links to city services. Town history remains useful secondary content.

## Product Purpose

An independent community guide that gets residents to useful local information quickly.

## Positioning

Owned by Twin Lakes Web Co. LLC as an independent community project; not operated by or associated with the City of Caneyville government.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing static HTML/CSS/JavaScript repository: twinlakeswebco/caneyville. Preserve directory entries, search and category filtering, useful external resource links, history topics, and responsive access. The user permits reorganizing, shortening, and rewriting generic copy. Do not invent events, photographs, business details, or official affiliations.

## Brand Commitments

Caneyville.org. Plain local language; remove AI slop. Credit Twin Lakes Web Co. LLC and use casey@twinlakesweb.com for corrections.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing index.html supplies 27 directory listings, resource URLs, historical content, community and school links. These are inherited content, not freshly verified business records. No local town photography exists in this checkout. The user supplied a Kentucky map; its transparent derivative is used as the home heading image, with its original linework and red Caneyville location marker retained.

## Product Principles

- Put residents' everyday tasks first.
- Keep information scannable and direct.
- Make independent ownership clear.
- Link to authoritative services rather than impersonating them.
